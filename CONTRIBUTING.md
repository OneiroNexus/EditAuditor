# Contributions

The project is a release draft. Reuse/contribution terms have not been selected.
This document does not grant rights or establish a contributor agreement.

Useful review contributions are synthetic failing cases, parser boundary reports,
tests for protected-token omissions, and clear distinctions between observed
behavior and desired behavior. Do not upload private documents, credentials,
personal data, or proprietary engine details.

No contribution creates authorship, inventorship, ownership or governance
authority by assertion. Any actual collaboration terms remain the owner's
separate, intentional decision.
